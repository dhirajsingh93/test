How we make your parts

From compound mixing to dispatch, with checks at every stage and a lab of our own. View full plant process →

01–04 / 12
  1. 01

    Requirement analysis

    Review your drawing, sample and where the part will be used.

  2. 02

    Material selection

    Choose from NBR, EPDM, HNBR, silicone, neoprene, natural rubber and more.

  3. 03

    Compound preparation

    Mix the compound to specification on our two-roll mixing mill.

  4. 04

    Tooling and mould setup

    Prepare the mould and keep it clean in our tool maintenance area.

  5. 05

    Moulding

    Compression moulding on hydraulic presses from 14″ to 25″ platens.

  6. 06

    Curing

    Cure each batch for the time and temperature its compound needs.

  7. 07

    Trimming and finishing

    Remove flash and clean up the edges of every part.

  8. 08

    Dimensional inspection

    Measure with vernier, micrometer, ring gauges and optical comparator.

  9. 09

    Lab testing

    Check hardness, tensile strength, ageing and cure behaviour in-house.

  10. 10

    Final approval

    Review the finished lot against your requirements before release.

  11. 11

    Packing

    Pack approved parts so they arrive clean and undamaged.

  12. 12

    Dispatch

    Ship on schedule from our plant in Faridabad, Haryana.
