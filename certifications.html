What ISO 9001:2015 stands for

ISO 9001 is the international standard for quality management systems. It does not approve a single product. It checks that a company has a working system to deliver the same quality every time, meet customer requirements and keep improving.

For Upadhyay Rubber Industries, that system covers the manufacture and supply of rubber and plastic components, from the first conversation about your drawing to the day your order is dispatched.

  • Customer focusYour requirements are understood before any work starts.
  • Clear processesEvery stage, from compound mixing to dispatch, follows a defined step.
  • Skilled peopleMotivation and training of employees is one of our quality objectives.
  • Facts, not guessworkParts are checked with calibrated instruments and in-house lab tests.
  • Steady improvementWe keep upgrading quality and process to meet changing customer needs.
