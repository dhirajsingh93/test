01 WHO WE ARE

Engineering strength.
Made for performance.

Upadhyay Rubber Industry delivers rubber and plastic component solutions for industrial and OEM applications. We focus on precision, material quality and the needs of each application.

From moulded components and industrial profiles to seals and gaskets, we work with customers to understand the part, its use and the material requirements behind it.

  • Application-first Solutions tailored to the component's purpose and requirements.
  • Material options NBR, EPDM, HNBR, silicone, neoprene and natural rubber.
  • Based in Faridabad Manufacturing and in-house testing in Faridabad, Haryana.
