In-House Polymer Compounding Capabilities

All compounds are formulated and mixed on our two-roll mixing mills to your exact hardness (Shore A), tensile strength, and elongation requirements.

NBR (Nitrile)

Nitrile Butadiene Rubber

-30°C to +120°C

Superior resistance to petroleum fuels, mineral oils, greases, and hydraulic fluids. The industry standard for oil seals, O-rings, and fuel gaskets.

EPDM

Ethylene Propylene Diene

-45°C to +150°C

Unmatched weather, ozone, UV, hot water, and steam resistance. Ideal for radiator hoses, electrical panel profiles, and vehicle door seals.

Silicone (VMQ)

High-Purity Silicone Rubber

-60°C to +230°C

Broadest thermal stability, outstanding electrical insulation, and non-toxic properties. Used in turbo hoses, masking plugs, and food/medical gaskets.

Viton (FKM)

Fluoroelastomer Rubber

-20°C to +250°C

Ultimate resistance to aggressive chemicals, synthetic aviation/engine lubricants, and extreme heat in severe industrial and automotive environments.

Neoprene (CR)

Chloroprene Rubber

-35°C to +120°C

Excellent tear resistance, mechanical toughness, and moderate oil/ozone resistance. Selected for dust boots, CV bellows, and machine pads.

Natural Rubber (NR)

Natural Gum Rubber

-40°C to +85°C

Superior tensile resilience, dynamic fatigue resistance, and shock absorption. The premier choice for bonded suspension bushes and engine mounts.
