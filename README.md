# Upadhyay Rubber Industries — Product Analysis & Sector Categorization

Comprehensive analysis and structured industry categorization for all rubber and plastic components manufactured by **Upadhyay Rubber Industries** (Faridabad, Haryana, India).

---

## 1. Executive Product Analysis

Upadhyay Rubber Industries specializes in precision rubber compounding, hydraulic compression moulding, profile extrusion, and rubber-to-metal bonding for OEM and tier-1 industrial clients.

### Core Product Classifications by Manufacturing Type:

1. **Anti-Vibration & Mounting Systems:**
   * Rubber bushes, torque rod bushes, suspension bushes, engine mounts, dampers, pads, and rubber-to-metal bonded isolation parts.
   * *Purpose:* Vibration isolation, shock absorption, NVH reduction, and angular deflection.

2. **Dynamic & Static Sealing Systems:**
   * O-Rings, Quad-Rings (X-Rings), U-Cups, V-Rings, rotary shaft oil seals, hydraulic packing seals, and back-up rings.
   * *Purpose:* Fluid containment (hydraulic oils, coolants, fuels, air, steam) preventing ingress of dust and dirt under varying temperatures and pressures.

3. **Gaskets & Flat Packings:**
   * Cylinder head gaskets, industrial flange gaskets, die-cut flat gaskets, end-cap gaskets, and sponge rubber sealing strips.
   * *Purpose:* Leak-free surface-to-surface sealing between bolted mechanical flanges and engine blocks.

4. **Protective Covers & Flexible Connectors:**
   * Dust covers, steering rack boots, CV joint bellows, cable grommets, and flexible drive couplings.
   * *Purpose:* Protecting moving rods, joints, ball studs, and wiring harnesses from abrasion, mud, road salt, moisture, and debris.

5. **Extruded Rubber Profiles & Hoses:**
   * Continuous solid/sponge EPDM profiles, edge trims, insulating sleeves, curved coolant hoses, and reinforced high-temp silicone hoses.
   * *Purpose:* Door/enclosure weatherproofing, wire bundle routing, and high-temperature pressurized fluid conveyance.

## 5. Developer Guide: How Products are Wired in Code

### Homepage 3-Row Continuous Marquee (`assets/js/home.js`)
* Products are defined in `productsData` array (starting around line 20).
* The script automatically divides products across the **3 horizontal rows** using modulo arithmetic (`idx % 3`):
  * **Row 1 (`prTrack1`):** Index `0, 3, 6, 9, 12, 15, 18, 21`
  * **Row 2 (`prTrack2`):** Index `1, 4, 7, 10, 13, 16, 19, 22`
  * **Row 3 (`prTrack3`):** Index `2, 5, 8, 11, 14, 17, 20, 23`
* Each row automatically clones its items for a continuous, gapless, 100% infinite marquee loop.

### How to Add a New Product to Any Sector:
To add a new product (e.g. for Agriculture or 4W), append an object to `productsData` in [`assets/js/home.js`](file:///c:/Users/dhira/Downloads/upadhyay/assets/js/home.js#L20):

```javascript
{
    title: "Tractor PTO Shaft Oil Seal",
    material: "NBR / HNBR",
    image: "images/oil-seal.jpg",
    cat: "oil-packing-seals"
}
```

The script automatically inserts it into the 3-row layout, maintains infinite marquee animation, and links directly to the category on [`products.html`](file:///c:/Users/dhira/Downloads/upadhyay/products.html).
